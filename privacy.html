Privacy Policy

Eksoch Seller Tools by Eksoch Analytics. Last updated: 24 November 2026.

What we collect

Nothing. Eksoch Analytics does not run a server for this app and never receives your data. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

Your keys

If you connect your Gumroad store or add an Anthropic API key, the key is saved only inside this app on your device. It is sent only to the service it belongs to:

  • Gumroad (api.gumroad.com), to show your products, sales and discount codes, and to make changes you confirm, such as creating a discount code.
  • Anthropic (api.anthropic.com), only when you click “Write listing”. The product details you type are sent so the AI can write the listing.

Gumroad's and Anthropic's own privacy policies apply to the data they process. Use of the AI writer is billed to your own Anthropic account.

Your Pro code

If you enter a Pro code (for example from AppSumo), it is checked inside the app on your device and saved only there. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Your sales data

Sales, buyer emails and product details are loaded from Gumroad into the app while you use it. They are not stored by us. A CSV export is saved only where you choose on your device.

Removing your data

Open Settings → Disconnect and remove all keys, or uninstall the app. You can also revoke the Gumroad token in Gumroad's settings and the API key in the Anthropic Console.

Contact

Eksoch Analytics: eksochanalytics.gumroad.com
